Turn Pacing — 3 players, 2 turns

Playtest configuration: 3 players, all Engineering, 2 turns.

TURN 1 (single shared expedition):
- All 3 players start at Crash Flats.
- One player takes Fabrication Lead (card 3, full role with private brief).
- One player takes the Power Systems Engineer Proxy from the Proxy Roles sheet.
- The third player plays without a role card this playtest — they will gain a Knowledge or Salvage hand card during play.
- Auto-draw cards 1-9 (narrative, dept, role select, phase ref, outpost). Auto-draw the ecosystem setup cards 10-12. Shuffle the remainder.
- Exploration: the team should comfortably draw cards 13 (Salvage Manifest), 14 (The Power Question), and 15 (Engine Core Section). Flipping card 15 requires 2 Oxygen + 1 Power — covered by Oxygen Tank (after upkeep), Fabrication Lead's Power, and Proxy's Power.
- Upkeep: craft 1-2 Aphelion Salvage items. Oxygen Tank (1 Materials + 1 Water) and Field Welder (2 Materials) are achievable from the starting Materials Reserve (3) plus the Coral Shelf / Wreckage Field tracks. If the team flipped Engine Core Section, the Thermal Power Generator becomes craftable on turn 2.

TURN 2 (two parallel expeditions, 3 players splitting 1+2):
- Expedition A (1 player, Reef Gardens): the Lead Ecologist or Knowledge-holding player heads to Reef Gardens. They auto-draw cards 1-12 to set up the location, take the Lead Ecologist/Systems Ecologist role from the deck, then explore cards 13-15. Reef Caverns (card 15) flip requires 2 Oxygen + 1 Knowledge — covered by Lead Ecologist (Oxygen) + Systems Ecologist hand version + Oxygen Tank if needed.
- Expedition B (2 players, Kelp Cathedral): both players carry hand cards from turn 1 (role + 1 salvage each). Entry requirement is 2 Oxygen, satisfied by Oxygen Tank + Lungweed/Lure Fish triggers. They explore the 10-card opening sequence.
- Crisis: Control plays The Aphelion Wraith (CH-1) at the end of turn 2 if any Megafauna Territory token has been placed (Watcher in the Dark in the Reef Caverns sub-deck is the most likely setup). Reactor Pulse (CH-2) is the secondary risk if option A was chosen in The Power Question.

WHY THIS PACES IN 2 TURNS:
- Crash Flats has only 11 actual cards (4 role slots deferred). Drawing all 11 in one turn is achievable if the team prioritises card 15 as their final flip.
- Reef Gardens with 1 player has 22 cards but most are auto-draws (1-12) and the rest are a normal 3-card exploration into cards 13-15. The 7-card Reef Cavern sub-deck is the player's actual content.
- Kelp Cathedral's 10 cards are an opening sequence — 6 of them auto-chain via 'Next:' instructions, leaving 4 cards in the shuffled pile to explore.
- 1 engineering proxy card (Power Systems Engineer) ensures the team always meets Power-icon gates without needing to wait for Fabrication Lead to be in two places at once.
