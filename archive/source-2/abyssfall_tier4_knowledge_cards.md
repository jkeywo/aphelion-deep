# Abyssfall — Tier 4 Knowledge Cards
*The Drowned Story Arc — five cards across three locations*

---

## Distribution

| # | Card Name | Location | Faction Voice | Story beat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Withheld Report | Pressure Cathedral | The Returners | The original sin — evidence suppressed |
| 2 | The Warden Descent | Black Coral Maze | The Wardens | The failed act — correction that worsened |
| 3 | Record 7: Peak Season | Bone Fields | The Harvesters | The last good record |
| 4 | Record 8: No Entry | Bone Fields | The Harvesters | The silence |

*Card 5 feeds directly into "What the Bones Know" (Bone Fields card 10), which reads aloud: "They did not die. They were moved…"*

---

## Design Notes

**Back face** — discovery prose. Second person, present tense, sensory and specific. ~100–120 words. No mechanics. Written as what the expedition physically encounters.

**Front face** — the record itself. Fields at top, revelation text below. ~80–100 words of body text. Register shifts by faction: Returners are precise and cold; Wardens are careful and fractured; Harvesters are confident then absent; Listeners are serene.

**Together** — the five cards read as a linear story of a civilisation that had the information, could not agree, acted badly, and finally, in one faction's case, did the thing that needed doing. Players who hold all five will understand what happened. Players who hold one or two will have a partial picture and something to trade.

# CARD 1 — THE WITHHELD REPORT

**Location:** Pressure Cathedral
**Story position:** 1 of 5

---

### BACK FACE
*(How it is found — second person, present tense)*

The upper chamber of the Pressure Cathedral has been searched three times already. The columns and the vertical shafts have given up biological systems, power relics, architectural data. You found what you were looking for.

This chamber is different. The walls here are not structural — they are inscribed. The substrate is biological, grown into the rock, and the inscription covers every surface at what would have been eye level for whatever stood here. It is not decorative. The spacing, the repetition, the density — this is a record. Someone spent a long time making sure this would last.

The substrate has partially degraded, but the central section is intact. Your instruments parse it in fragments. What comes through first is a table of figures. Then a date. Then a gap. Then a single line, repeated four times, that the translation renders as:

*We did not share this in time.*

# CARD 2 — THE WARDEN DESCENT

**Location:** Black Coral Maze
**Story position:** 2 of 5

---

### BACK FACE
*(How it is found — second person, present tense)*

The Black Coral Maze is not a maze by accident. The corridors branch and recurse, and the coral itself has grown to fill what were once straight passages. You have been following a single thread of markings — scratched into the coral at knee height, easy to miss — for two hours before the passage opens into a chamber that was clearly not part of the original structure.

It was added. Hastily, by the looks of the construction — less elegant than everything else here, more urgent. The marks on the wall are in a different medium, a different hand. Not grown into the substrate. Cut.

There is a list of names. Below the names, a plan — detailed, technical, annotated. At the bottom, a single line added after the plan was complete, in a heavier cut:

*The Depth did not answer.*

The cage was weaker when they were done than when they began."*

# CARD 3 — RECORD 7: PEAK SEASON

**Location:** Bone Fields
**Story position:** 3 of 5

---

### BACK FACE
*(How it is found — second person, present tense)*

The Bone Fields read as a graveyard, but the structures built among the remains are not mourning — they are industrial. Whatever civilisation worked here was processing the bones for materials: the calcified lattices, the marrow channels, the mineral deposits in the larger remains. They were very good at it.

The records here are different from the inscribed walls of the Cathedral. These are smaller — tablets, dense with notation, stacked in a recess that has survived because the coral sealed over it and the pressure preserved what the coral enclosed. You break it open and the tablets are intact.

They are numbered. You have seven and eight. You look for the others. They are not here.

Record 7 is marked in the Drowned equivalent of red.

---

*(The record — card fields + revelation text)*

**Deck:** Bone Fields
**Card #:** [n]
**Name:** Record 7: Peak Season
**Type:** Knowledge
**Tier:** 4
**Unlock Cost:** Draw from open pool

**In Play / Effect:** Take immediately into hand on draw.

# CARD 4 — RECORD 8: NO ENTRY

**Location:** Bone Fields
**Story position:** 4 of 5

---

### BACK FACE
*(How it is found — second person, present tense)*

Record 8 is the last tablet in the stack. It is shorter than the others — not a full season's record. The notation in the upper section is standard: date, site identifiers, personnel. The middle section is where the format breaks. The notation becomes irregular, then sparse, then absent. There is a long blank section on the tablet's face that was never inscribed.

At the bottom, in the same hand as the opening notation — careful, professional, the hand of someone who filled out forms for a living — there are three lines.

The translation renders them as:

*Site 4 is not responding.*
*Site 7 is not responding.*
*[Untranslatable — instrument analogue, possibly: the reading is higher than the scale.]*

That is all.

---

*(The record — card fields + revelation text)*

**Deck:** Bone Fields
**Card #:** [n]
**Name:** Record 8: No Entry
**Type:** Knowledge
**Tier:** 4
**Unlock Cost:** Draw from open pool

**In Play / Effect:** Take immediately into hand on draw.

**Hand Version**
*Reusable:* Once per turn, ask Control: "Is the Woken's activation threshold close?" Control answers with one of three responses: Not yet / Approaching / Imminent. Control answers honestly.
*Trash:* Remove all Warning Tokens from the map. The threshold resets. This does not repair the cage — it only clears the accumulated signal. The Woken is still active.

**Notes — read aloud on draw:**
*"Record 8 is dated eleven days after Record 7. There are no Records 9 through whatever the final number was. The recess that held the tablets has space for more. They were never placed there. Whether the author stopped writing, or stopped being able to, or simply stopped — the record does not say. The last legible mark on the tablet is the incomplete notation at the top of a third section that was never filled in."*

*Control does not place a Warning Token. Control notes who in the room goes quiet.*

# CARD 5 — FINAL TRANSMISSION

**Location:** Bone Fields
**Story position:** 5 of 5

*This card feeds directly into "What the Bones Know" (Bone Fields card 10). The two cards should be drawn in sequence if at all possible — consider placing this card immediately before the final Knowledge card in the deck ordering.*

---

### BACK FACE
*(How it is found — second person, present tense)*

This one is not with the tablets. It is not inscribed or cut or grown. It is transmitted — a signal, low frequency, still broadcasting on a band your instruments were not calibrated to receive until you were this deep, this close. You almost missed it.

When you isolate the frequency, it resolves into something that is not quite language and not quite not. Your instruments render it as text because that is the closest available category. The rendering is incomplete and probably wrong in places.

But the last line comes through clearly. It has been broadcasting for a very long time, waiting for someone with the right instruments to be in the right place.

You are the first.

## Notes for card layout

**Print sequencing:** Cards 3, 4, and 5 share a location and will often be drawn in order. Their emotional arc — satisfaction, absence, transmission — depends on that sequence. Where possible, place them consecutively in the Bone Fields draw pool rather than scattered through it.

**Card 5 and "What the Bones Know":** The existing Bone Fields final card reads aloud: *"They did not die. They were moved…"* — this is the Listeners' story, told at one remove. Card 5 (Final Transmission) tells the same story directly, from inside. The two cards should be felt as the same revelation arriving twice: once as archaeological inference, once as a voice.

**Warning Token note:** Cards 3 and 4 interact with Warning Tokens asymmetrically — Card 3 adds one; Card 4 removes none and notes the silence instead. This is intentional. The Harvester story does not clean up after itself.

**Philosophy reveals:** Cards 1 and 2 include Control notes about player reactions. These are the richest philosophy-surfacing moments in the Tier 4 arc. Facilitate them deliberately — slow the pace, let the read-aloud land, watch the table.
