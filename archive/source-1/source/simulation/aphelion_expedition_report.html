What the sim reveals

The colony is thriving on resources — production and life-support both climb every turn — yet it reaches defeat critical by turn 3. The Fabrication Lead's extraction at Crash Flats, right beside the sunken Engine Core, stokes the reactor an extra phase each turn, and with both ecologists committed to the reef and the deep, no one is ever assigned to contain the reactor. Two restorers to one exploiter keeps the megafauna calm (aggression stays at 0), so the danger isn't the sea — it's the clock the extraction feeds.

Lever to test. The assignment policy optimises for colony supply and never trades an expedition slot for attempt_stabilise_reactor. Add a reactor-need term (e.g. when phase ≥ 3, pull the engineer off Crash Flats to contain) and re-run — the harness will show whether three players is simply too few to both supply the colony and hold the clock.
