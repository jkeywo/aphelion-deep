Reef Gardens — a faithful expedition

The real 21-card ordered, two-faced deck, turned one card at a time by the package's own mechanisms. Dispositions, Population starts, and upkeep bands are read from the workbook; the deck cursor persists across turns. This is the game's actual rule loop — not the invented stand-in.

The gate holds. Turning the surface reef (cards 1–14) reaches the Reef Caverns expedition marker, which requires 2 Oxygen + 2 Knowledge to descend. The surface reef yields only 1 Oxygen (Filterweed at Pop 3+) and 1 Knowledge (one notes card) — so the deep stays locked across all three turns, the cursor resting on the marker. The earlier stand-in let anyone dive anywhere; the encoded rules gate the deep behind ecosystem development, exactly as designed. (Whether the second Knowledge card sitting *behind* the gate is intended or a content snag is now a visible, testable question.)

Upkeep encoding coverage — the honest gap

Of 46 cards, 19 have an Upkeep section; only 4 use the clean Pop N – effect form. Of those, Filterweed encodes fully; the population-dynamics fauna parse their bands but their effect ops (consume-from-fauna, else-branches) need hand-encoding; the rest (Rations, Choose-one, convert/produce) are free-form prose. Each unencoded card is flagged, not silently skipped — the tracked gap the executable layer exists to surface.
