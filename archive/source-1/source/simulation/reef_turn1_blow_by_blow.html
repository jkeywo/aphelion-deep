The gate moment — is the icon count right?

The Reef Caverns gate needs 2 Oxygen + 2 Knowledge (present in hand or at the location; not consumed).

What the current sim counted: oxygen 0, knowledge 1 → closed. It missed the two role passives and mis-handled the knowledge card, so it under-counted.

What your rules actually allow this turn:
· Oxygen = 2 ✓ — Lead Ecologist passive (1) + Filterweed pushed to Pop 3 by the Systems Ecologist’s Cultivator, which then provides 1. The Oxygen requirement is reachable turn 1.
· Knowledge = 1 ✗ — the group faces a real dilemma: choose Protect and Filterweed→5, Grazers→4 so two cards reach 4+ Pop and you gain the Notes card (1 Knowledge); or choose Tag & Monitor for 1 Knowledge directly — but not both. Either path yields only 1 of the 2 Knowledge needed.

So the cavern stays closed on turn 1 — but as a tight, intended puzzle (Oxygen met, Knowledge one short), not the “permanently locked” picture the un-corrected sim implied. Whether a second Knowledge source should exist before the gate is now a concrete design question.
