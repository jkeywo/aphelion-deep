The Aphelion Deep — a 3-player, 3-turn game

All three Tier-1 locations now run from their card capability blocks — Reef Gardens, and the two just converted, Crash Flats (14 cards) and Kelp Cathedral (9). One interpreter (mechanisms/card_effects.py) turns each ordered, two-faced deck; roles, gates, upkeep, crafting and yields are all read from the data. The team states a plan each turn; every consequence below is computed, not scripted. Decks persist between turns. Over three turns the crafted salvage opens two of the three deep sites — no hypotheticals, this is the real game loop.

The cast. One Engineer and two Ecologists. Nobody starts with a role — the department-reveal cards assign them as the decks are turned: Player 1 becomes Fabrication Lead (passive Power icon), Players 2 & 3 become Lead Ecologist (passive Oxygen) and Systems Ecologist (the Cultivator action).

The three-turn arc — the economy is a lock, salvage is the key. Turn 1 every expedition reaches its gate and it holds: Reef Caverns want 2 Oxygen + 2 Knowledge (the reef yields 2 & 1), the Engine Core wants 1 Oxygen + 3 Power (the wreck makes Power but no Oxygen), Kelp needs 2 Oxygen to enter. But the wreck's Materials pay for salvage: in the team phase the Engineer crafts the Oxygen Tank (1 Material → Oxygen) and the Scan Database (3 Power on hand → Knowledge). Turn 2 he carries the Oxygen Tank to Kelp; with Lead Ecologist's Oxygen that makes 2 — Kelp Cathedral opens and its nine cards turn. Turn 3 all three converge on the Reef Caverns: the Oxygen Tank, the Scan Database and Lead Ecologist's Notes pool with the reef's own Filterweed Oxygen to reach 3 Oxygen + 2 Knowledge — the caverns open and cards 16–21 turn. Two of the three deep sites cracked in three turns, entirely through crafted salvage and coordination — exactly what the recipes and roles are for. Only the Engine Core stays sealed (it wants the Power the team spent elsewhere) — a clean, legible trade-off, not a dead end.
A food-chain question the encoding surfaces. Once the Reef Caverns open in Turn 3, the Crystalblind Snapper (apex predator, eats Prey) and the surface Spotted Grazers (eat Fauna) share a board; as written the Grazers can eat the Snapper. And Kelp's Giant Lungweed reaches only Pop 2 with no Cultivator present, so its Pressure icon (Pop 3+) never lights — the icon economy is sensitive to who is on the expedition. Both are concrete, testable content notes, not sim bugs.
Where this leaves the conversion. All three locations are now executable data. gsx L6 validates every verb, icon, track and reference across the three files (0 errors, 12 honest unresolved gaps). New this round and now in the closed vocabulary: the Pressure icon and Scanning track, keyword-scoped conditions (keyword_track_ge, keyword_present, keyword_count_ge, not), a conversion rate (the Wreckage Field's 1 Abundance → 2 Materials), and a craft economy (cost / requires → hand or tuck) that turns salvage into icons in the team phase. The power/energy icon is unified to power as confirmed. Upkeep resolves in deck order; the Phase Reference card says players choose the order, so that is one valid ordering — a knob the report makes visible.
